🌱 Been there: felt spiritually numb at 18

At 18 I felt exactly this. I'd stand in worship and feel like a fraud. What I learned: faith isn't measured by how much we feel. The fact that you care about this says something real is still there.

"Why, my soul, are you downcast? … Put your hope in God, for I will yet praise him."Psalm 42:5
